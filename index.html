Credits and license

Pocket Retro is an unofficial personal app (version 1.5.1). Its Tape effect engine is a JavaScript port of the core effect from ntsc-rs by valadaptive, which is itself based on ntscqt, ntsc and composite-video-simulator. The port follows the original's filters and settings, but noise patterns and some details differ. TV rating icons are the US TV Parental Guidelines marks you supplied.
